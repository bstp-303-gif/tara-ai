Step 1 — Compile & Rank (as yourself/MoE uploader)
Go to http://localhost:8000/agents/agent1-dashboard/, scroll to the green "📋 Compile & Rank Applications" card, and click Compile & Rank (1 pending). This scores the application, ranks it within Johor, and flags it Recommended if it fills a district/tech quota. Status moves to Compiled.

Step 2 — Reset the Johor officer's password yourself (since I can't safely hand you that one — it got lost earlier)
Go to http://localhost:8000/admin/, log in as admin, click Users → johor_officer → the "this form" link next to the password field → set a new password. Django admin doesn't need the old one, so this is entirely under your control.

Step 3 — Act as the State Officer
Log out of admin, go to http://localhost:8000/agents/login/, sign in as johor_officer with the password you just set. You'll land on State Review — Johor, see the candidate with her rank/score, and click Approve (add a remark if you like). Status moves to State Approved.

Step 4 — Act as the MoE Officer (you)
Log out, log back in as moe_officer / testpass123 (worth changing this password the same way, via admin, once you're done testing). You'll land on MoE Review, see the same candidate now — with her state, rank, and score — and can Approve (this fires the real Letter of Recognition email to her address) or Decline.

Try it in that order and let me know what you see at each step — happy to adjust anything (page layout, what info is shown, wording) once you've clicked through it.
